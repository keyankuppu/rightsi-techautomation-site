Case studies

Real plants. Real constraints. Real results.

Connecting a Legacy Production Line

Client: mid-sized automotive parts manufacturer, 3 production lines
Challenge

No real-time visibility into machine downtime or throughput. Supervisors relied on end-of-shift paper logs, making it nearly impossible to identify recurring bottlenecks.

Solution

A floor-level connectivity audit, followed by a non-invasive IoT sensor retrofit across three production lines, with data integrated into a live dashboard for operators and management — all without halting production.

Results
  • Real-time visibility across 3 lines within 8 weeks
  • 22% reduction in unplanned downtime within the first quarter
  • Bottleneck identification cut from days to minutes

MES Rollout for a Food & Beverage Plant

Client: regional food processing company, single facility
Challenge

Production data lived in spreadsheets and disconnected systems, causing reporting delays and traceability gaps that created risk during audits.

Solution

Selection and configuration of an MES platform tailored to the plant's batch processes, integrated with the existing ERP, with supervisors and operators trained through a phased go-live.

Results
  • Full batch traceability achieved within one production cycle
  • Reporting time cut from days to same-shift
  • Audit preparation time reduced by roughly 60%

Smart Factory Pilot to Full-Line Rollout

Client: consumer goods manufacturer, single pilot line
Challenge

Leadership wanted to modernize but was hesitant to commit facility-wide investment without proof of concept.

Solution

Design and commissioning of a smart factory pilot on one line, incorporating adaptive control logic and automated material handling, with clear KPIs to evaluate before scaling.

Results
  • Pilot line achieved measurable throughput gains within 90 days
  • Findings used to build the business case for a 4-line facility rollout
  • Rollout phased over 12 months to protect ongoing production
